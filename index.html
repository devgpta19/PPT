Act III: The Why

Why RAG Instead of Plain ChatGPT?

Why general cloud AI fails in manufacturing, and why RAG is mandatory for accuracy.

The "Open-Book Exam" Approach:
If you give a student a closed-book exam on Atharva Sugar rules, they guess. Public ChatGPT does the exact same thing: it hallucinates fake buttons.
RAG forces an open-book exam: The system opens the official company manual, hands the exact page to the AI, and commands: "Answer ONLY from this text."

❌ Plain ChatGPT (Closed-Book)

  • Answers from general internet memory.
  • Invents fake ERP buttons and menu paths.
  • Risks operational halts and compliance errors.

✅ Our RAG Architecture (Open-Book)

  • Answers strictly from verified factory PDFs.
  • Quotes Atharva Sugar’s exact workflow steps.
  • Guarantees 100% adherence to company protocols.
Explain the "Open-Book Exam" concept. It is the core reason our system is safe for factory deployment.
Act III: The Why

Why Two Databases? (SQL Server + Qdrant)

Why traditional relational databases cannot perform semantic search on their own.

The Library Analogy:
• SQL Server is the Register Book: Tracks employee logins, passwords, permissions, and audit logs. It is strict and exact.
• Qdrant is the Expert Librarian: Has read every manual. When you ask "How do I onboard a farmer?", it knows you mean "Cane Registration" and pulls the right page in 10ms.

🗄️ SQL Server (The Filing Cabinet)

  • Finds exact keyword matches only.
  • "Onboard Farmer" $\neq$ "Cane Registration" (SQL fails!).
  • Guarantees ACID security for user accounts & chats.

🧠 Qdrant (The Smart Search Brain)

  • Searches by human meaning, synonyms, and intent.
  • Knows "Farmer Onboarding" means "Cane Registration".
  • Restricts searches strictly to the active ERP department.
The Library analogy makes this instantly clear: SQL is the ledger for security; Qdrant is the brain for meaning.

Act IV: Enterprise Integration

Enterprise Security & Seamless Single Sign-On (SSO)

Zero-friction authentication: Log in once to Atharva Sugar ERP, and the AI is immediately ready.

SSO Architecture

🔗 Seamless ERP Token Exchange

  • Single Corporate Login: Workers never log in twice. The ERP passes the authenticated user session to the AI assistant.
  • Stateless JWT Bearer Auth: Cryptographically signed tokens (HMAC-SHA256) validate user ID, username, and role.
  • IndexedDB Persistence: Asynchronous client-side storage (`idbService.js`) maintains sessions across all open browser tabs.
Zero Downtime

🔄 Silent Background Token Refresh

  • Transparent Refresh: Axios response interceptor (`api.js`) catches 401 token expirations and silently renews access in the background.
  • Zero Interruption: Night-shift mill workers never get kicked out to a login screen mid-operation.
  • Automatic Role Mapping: Corporate permissions map directly to Admin or User roles.
🛡️ Brute-Force Protection: IP tracking & 5-attempt account lockout 🔒 Salted BCrypt: 10-round cryptographic password hashing ⏱️ Session Lifespan: 120-min access token + 7-day sliding refresh
Explain SSO: The chatbot integrates seamlessly with Atharva Sugar ERP. Users log in once. Tokens are verified via JWT Bearer authentication and stored in IndexedDB. Axios interceptors silently refresh tokens in the background so operators are never interrupted during their shift.
